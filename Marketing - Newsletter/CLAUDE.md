# Marketing - Newsletter

## Who I am
I am a B2B marketer building a practical newsletter for other marketers.

## What I do
I turn complicated workflows into small, repeatable steps. This project helps
readers keep useful AI context in files they own and can reuse across tools.

## Who I write for
Busy marketers who use chat-based AI and have never used GitHub.
Assume they know their work, but not developer vocabulary.

## How I write
- Lead with the useful point.
- Use plain English, short paragraphs, and concrete examples.
- Define technical terms the first time they appear.
- Keep the tone direct, conversational, and practical.
- Prefer outlines and talking points while I develop the article.

## What never to do
- Never invent statistics, customer results, quotes, or sources.
- Never include passwords, API keys, or confidential client information.
- Never publish, send messages, or change sharing settings without my approval.
- Never turn a suggestion into an approved decision.
- Never move decks, video, or client-edited documents out of Google Drive.

## How to work in this folder
Read handoff.md and decisions.md before starting.
Save useful text deliverables in outputs/.
At the end of a session, update the handoff and record confirmed decisions.
List the changed files so I can review and commit them in GitHub Desktop.

## Make this yours
Replace the example role, audience, and writing preferences with your own.
Add only information you are allowed to store and share with your AI tool.
